The Process

Three Stages of Medical Device Manufacturing

Swipe to see contamination-control needs by production stage.

ISO 5–8

Plastics Molding & Extrusion

Machines melt, inject, extrude, and cool plastic resins to produce medical device subcomponents.
Recommend: short-cuff gloves + polyester/nonwoven wiper combo to control contamination during production and handling.
ISO 4–6

Device Assembly

Operators bond, weld, and laser subcomponents together into a finished device.
Recommend: long-cuff gloves + critical polyester wipers + protective apparel — direct product contact is increasing here.
ISO 3–5

Final Configuration & Clean Packaging

Finished devices are configured into kits, packaged, and prepared for sterilization and patient use.
Recommend: long-cuff gloves + critical wipers + protective apparel to maintain integrity through final inspection and packaging.
