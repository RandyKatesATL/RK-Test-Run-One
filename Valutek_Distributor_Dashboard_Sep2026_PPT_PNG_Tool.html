Definitions and notes
Period
YTD is January 1 to September 30 by invoice date. 2025 and 2024 YTD use the same Jan-Sep window. Q3 is Jul-Sep.
Sales basis
Product sales only (product classes 1 to 9). Freight, rebates, tax and restock lines are excluded.
Channels
Strategic is Fastenal, Hisco/TestEquity and RS Hughes. Opportunistic is a named list of 15 distributors. Other is every remaining distributor.
Percent floors
Percent rankings include only distributors with $10K or more of 2025 YTD sales. Accounts with no 2025 sales show as new.
Tie-out
Strategic ties to the All Distributors summary to the dollar. The total differs by about 0.03%, from a few accounts where the draft summary differs from the invoice file.
Left out
October 2026 invoices. Product families 8 and 9 are under $1K.

Source: Valutek Distributor Rankings workbook, Sep 2026 YTD (product sales, Jan 1 to Sep 30 by invoice date).
