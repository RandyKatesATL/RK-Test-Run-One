Tier Definitions
Tier 1 Strategic Commercial

Roles with direct authority to land, expand, or influence large regional and national accounts. These are the hunters and strategic account managers — CSCs, KADS, NASS, BSS, and RSMs — who operate above the branch and drive enterprise-level growth. Tier 1 contacts are largely absent from the current list and represent the most important relationship gap to close. When a Tier 1 contact engages Valutek, drop everything and respond same day with your best resource.

Tier 2 Gateway — District Authority

Roles with accountability for both branch performance and outside sales teams, including indirect direction of CSCs. District Managers are the connective tissue between branch execution and strategic growth. A single converted DM influences multiple GMs and CSCs simultaneously, creating a multiplier effect no amount of individual branch calls can replicate. Tier 2 contacts are the bridge to Tier 1 — every DM conversation should include a direct ask for the CSC covering cleanroom or semiconductor accounts in their district.

Tier 3 Branch Foundation

Roles that operate at the branch level, including General Managers, Outside Sales Representatives, and Account Specialists. GMs optimize current business and set branch culture but are not actively hunting new accounts. OSRs are the primary field-selling resource and should be armed with a customer-ready cleanroom story. Account Specialists own book-of-business accounts and are systematically overlooked by manufacturers — they already have cleanroom customers and need only a single talking point to open the conversation. Tier 3 is the foundation of pull-through revenue and the intelligence network for reaching Tier 2 and Tier 1.
