DESIGN PRINCIPLES — PART 1

Start with the buyer, not the deck

Focused Message

One slide, one idea. Every extra bullet is a tax on comprehension. If a slide needs a paragraph to explain it, it's two slides wearing a trench coat.

Customer Insight

Your buyer doesn't care how the product works — they care what breaks if they don't fix it. Build the message from their problem, not your spec sheet.

DESIGN PRINCIPLES — PART 2

The two rules that do the heavy lifting

Contrast

Size, weight, and color aren't decoration — they're a hierarchy. If everything is emphasized, nothing is. Let one element on each slide carry the weight.

Proximity

Related things sit close together; unrelated things get space. Most "cluttered" decks aren't over-designed — they're just missing this one rule.
