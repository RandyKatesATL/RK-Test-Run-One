How the Mat Works
Disposable polyethylene film sheets coated with pressure-sensitive adhesive. Dirt sticks. Peel the layer. Done. 30 or 60 sheets per mat. Corner tabs are consecutively numbered so you always know how many layers remain.
01
Mat Tack Level
The “tack” is the stickiness — adhesive strength precisely balanced during manufacturing. Too weak and particles don’t transfer from the shoe. Valutek engineers this balance for optimal capture across all mat sizes.
02
Adhesive Softness
If adhesive is too hard, particles bounce off instead of sticking. If too soft, adhesive transfers to the shoe or wheel — defeating the purpose. Valutek’s formulation hits the window exactly.
03
Film & Adhesive Thickness
Adhesive layers run 2–4mm (50–100 microns). Too thin and particles stay on the surface, shortening mat life. Valutek’s 0.05 micron film thickness is thicker than competitors — by design.

Rep Sizing Rule — Say This Every Time
“Match the doorway width with one mat — never tile two smaller mats side by side.” Tiling creates a gap at the joint. Particles walk through it. One mat, right size, every time.
Critical Sizing Rule: Never Tile
Avoid placing two smaller mats side-by-side at a doorway — this allows particles through the joint. Always span the full entrance with one mat. When changing sheets, start at the tabbed corner closest to the doorway and roll the waste film into a ball as you go.
